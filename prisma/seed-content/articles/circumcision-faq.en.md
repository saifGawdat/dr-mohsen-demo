Circumcision is one of the most common childhood operations, and the difference between a smooth experience and a frightening one is almost never the hand speed — it is the assessment before and the follow-up after. These are the questions we answer every day.

### When should it be done?

- It can be done at **any age** — from the newborn to the adult.
- The most common window is **6 months to 5 years** — easy to explain, and recovery is quick.
- Older boys do fine as well; recovery simply takes a little longer.

### Is the anaesthetic safe?

Yes — with a paediatric anaesthetist, modern anaesthesia is weight- and age-adjusted and its risks are very low. What usually frightens parents is not the technique but the chatter around it — ask the doctor exactly which technique will be used and why it fits your child.

### Before the operation

- **Fasting by age:** breast milk 4 hours — formula or a light meal 6 hours — a normal meal 8 hours.
- **Labs:** a CBC and coagulation screen at minimum, plus whatever the surgeon orders for your child's age.
- **A cold, flu or active jaundice means postpone** — we wait until the child is completely well.

### After the operation

- Mild soreness for the first two days is normal; pain relief runs on a schedule — the child never has to earn it by crying first.
- A pale red or light-yellow surface on the wound is normal healing. **Dark purple colouring, an odour, or continuous bleeding — contact us immediately.**
- Stitches usually dissolve within 2–4 weeks.
- Nursery or school is generally fine after 3–5 days; swimming and rough play wait **2 weeks**.

### The one thing to check first: hypospadias

- If the urine opening is **not at the tip of the penis** — or there is a clear bend — **do not circumcise before seeing a paediatric surgeon**. The skin at the tip is exactly the tissue used to rebuild the urethra in the hypospadias repair; cutting it too early makes that operation harder later. That is why in this clinic, examining the tip is the first step of every circumcision assessment, not an exception.

**The main idea:** a short delay costs nothing, and a proper examination is worth everything. If anything feels unusual before the visit, message us first.
